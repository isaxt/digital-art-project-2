`entities.json`, `sources.json` and `events.json` here are the seven records (Kara Walker, Dropbox, Airbnb, Aesthetic, EveryArt, the Met, Y Combinator) gathered earlier from public pages through an automated page summary. They did NOT come from the two supplied datasets and were never checked by a person, so they are no longer loaded by the app. Nothing here is imported or displayed.

To restore any of them, each fact must first be verified against its source and given an explicit, documented provenance.
